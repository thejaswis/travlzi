# Interlaken
3-days Christmas get away to Interlaken.

Interlaken is one of the towns which attracts tourists for different reasons in every season. From paragliding in summer to Christmas markets in winter, it makes us fall in love with this small town in Switzerland.

## How to Land
* **Zurich:** 2-hour journey by train, featuring an international airport for foreign travelers.
* **Bern:** The capital of Switzerland, connected by a 45-minute train journey.
* **Geneva:** 2.5 hours away with direct trains.

## Christmas Itinerary
The whole city is covered with snow and vibrant lights from late November onward. The Christmas decorations are an eye-candy for viewers.

### Day 1: Jungfrau - Top of Europe
* **Morning:** Start your day early around 9:00 AM. 
* **Journey:** Board the train from Interlaken Ost. After 2 transits and a 2-hour scenic journey, you will arrive at the Top of Europe.
* **Activities:** Enjoy the snow-filled Aletsch Glacier. The **Ice Palace** is a main attraction here—kids will love watching all the ice-sculpted statues. 
* **Evening:** Spend a few hours and return to Interlaken.

### Day 2: Grindelwald - First
* **Morning:** Start your day from Interlaken Ost with a direct mountain train, then take the cable car to reach the mountain.
* **Key Attractions:** 
  * You must try the **First Cliff Walk by Tissot**, a viewing platform that runs around the Eiger rocky mountain.
  * If you love adventure, the **First Flyer**, **First Glider**, **First Mountain Cart**, and **First Trottibike Scooter** are must-tries (queues for activities depend on the season).
* **Afternoon:** Go hiking around the mountain to capture beautiful pictures, and try out the cafe on the mountain for lunch.
* **Evening:** Head back to Interlaken.

### Day 3: Roam Around the Town
* **Shopping:** Explore the many souvenir shops just behind Interlaken Ost, which also features numerous Swiss watch shops.
* **Christmas Market:** Visit the **Kursaal Garden** in the center of the city, which hosts a market where you can shop for Christmas decorations, sweet treats, and handicrafts.

## Food to Try
* **Gelato:** Excellent gelato shops in the center of town, such as **Gelateria Azzurra** and **Bistro Bella Ciao**.
* **Winter Classics:** Hot fondue, raclette, roasted chestnuts, gingerbread, and warm mulled wine (**Glühwein**) are must-tries.
* **Groceries:** Stop by **Coop** to buy quick snacks and fresh fruits.

## Pro Tip
Paragliding in Interlaken is a once-in-a-lifetime experience. Many local agencies arrange this, but **pre-booking is mandatory**. It doesn't involve any free fall, and the scenic beauty is to die for.
