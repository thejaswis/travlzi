SWITZERLAND · A THREE-DAY ESCAPE

Interlaken,
your alpine story.

Set between Lake Thun and Lake Brienz, Interlaken is a base for big alpine views, scenic rail journeys and seasonal experiences. Visit in December for a festive atmosphere, but remember that snow in town and mountain activity schedules depend on the weather.

BEFORE YOU GO

Getting to Interlaken

Interlaken is easy to reach by rail from Switzerland’s main cities and airports. Times below are planning estimates, not guaranteed schedules; check your travel date and connection before booking.

Zurich

Around 2 hours on many services. Zurich Airport is a convenient international gateway; allow extra time for airport transfers and connections.

Bern

Roughly 45–60 minutes on many services from Switzerland’s capital, depending on the train.

Geneva

Usually a longer rail journey than from Zurich or Bern. Check the current timetable for the fastest connection and any changes.

THE THREE-DAY PLAN

A high-alpine day, a mountain village, and festive town walks.

01

Jungfraujoch — Top of Europe

Morning: Leave early from Interlaken Ost and travel via Grindelwald or Lauterbrunnen. The journey to Jungfraujoch takes roughly 1.5–2 hours each way, depending on the route. It is a full-day outing, so check the forecast and timetable before you go.

  • Enjoy the panoramic views of the Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau and look towards the Aletsch Glacier from the viewing areas.
  • Explore the Ice Palace and other open attractions, subject to operating conditions.
  • Dress in warm layers, wear shoes with good grip, use sun protection and take it slowly at altitude.
02

Grindelwald-First — views and adventure

Morning: Take the train to Grindelwald and check whether the First gondola and summit attractions are operating on your date. Winter conditions can change the available route and activities.

  • If open, walk the First Cliff Walk by Tissot for dramatic views towards the Eiger.
  • Do not assume the First Flyer, First Glider, Mountain Cart or Trottibike Scooter will run in winter; these activities are seasonal and weather-dependent.
  • If mountain attractions are closed, enjoy Grindelwald village, a suitable marked winter walk or a relaxed café stop instead.

Check the official Grindelwald-First information for operating status, activity restrictions and current conditions before setting out.

03

Explore Interlaken town

Keep the final day relaxed and enjoy the town at your own pace.

  • Browse souvenir shops and Swiss watch stores around the town and near Interlaken Ost.
  • Look for seasonal market stalls, festive decorations, sweet treats and handmade gifts. Markets are held at different locations around the wider Interlaken holiday region, so check the official calendar rather than assuming every market is in the town centre.
  • Take a gentle walk, enjoy the mountain views and leave time for a warm café stop.

Browse the official Interlaken Christmas-market calendar for confirmed dates and locations for your travel year.

A TASTE OF SWITZERLAND

What to eat and where to pause

Gelato stops

Look out for Gelateria Azzurra and Bistro Bella Ciao in central Interlaken; check current opening times.

Winter comfort food

Warm up with Swiss fondue, raclette, roasted chestnuts and gingerbread when they’re available.

Easy essentials

Pick up water, snacks and fresh fruit at a Coop supermarket before a day trip. Cafés are also a good stop for hot chocolate after a cold walk.

✳

Travlzi tip: plan for the season, not just the photos

Reserve popular mountain journeys ahead of time, but keep your schedule flexible. Mountain weather can change quickly, and some lifts, trails and activities run seasonally. Check official operating updates, leave room for travel delays, and keep a town-based backup plan for poor weather.
