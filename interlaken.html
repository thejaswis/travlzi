BEFORE YOU GO

Getting to Interlaken

Interlaken is well connected by train. These approximate journey times are a useful starting point; check current timetables and connections before travelling.

Zurich

About 2 hours by train. Zurich Airport is a major international arrival point.

Bern

About 45 minutes by train from Switzerland’s capital.

Geneva

About 2.5 hours on some direct train services; verify the schedule for your date.

THE THREE-DAY PLAN

Snowy peaks, mountain views
and a little town time.

01

Jungfraujoch — Top of Europe

Morning: Start around 9:00 AM from Interlaken Ost. The scenic rail journey usually involves changes and takes around two hours, depending on the connection.

  • Take in the high-alpine views and the Aletsch Glacier area.
  • Visit the Ice Palace and see its ice sculptures, subject to opening and access conditions.
  • Allow time for the altitude, weather and return journey before heading back to Interlaken.
02

Grindelwald-First — views and adventure

Morning: Travel from Interlaken Ost towards Grindelwald, then take the cable car to First when operating.

  • Walk the First Cliff Walk by Tissot for dramatic views around the mountain.
  • Depending on the season and weather, look into the First Flyer, First Glider, Mountain Cart or Trottibike Scooter.
  • Choose a suitable trail, pause for lunch at a mountain café, and return to Interlaken in the evening.

Check lift operations, activity age/height requirements, weather and queue updates before setting out.

03

Explore Interlaken town

Keep the final day relaxed and enjoy the town at your own pace.

  • Browse souvenir shops and Swiss watch stores around the town and near Interlaken Ost.
  • Visit Kursaal Garden and look for seasonal Christmas-market stalls, decorations, sweet treats and handmade gifts.
  • Take a gentle walk, enjoy the mountain views and leave time for a warm café stop.

Christmas markets and decorations are seasonal; check dates and locations for your travel year.

A TASTE OF SWITZERLAND

What to eat and where to pause

Gelato stops

Look out for Gelateria Azzurra and Bistro Bella Ciao in central Interlaken; check current opening times.

Winter comfort food

Try Swiss fondue, raclette, roasted chestnuts and gingerbread when they’re available.

Easy essentials

Coop supermarkets are useful for quick snacks, drinks and fresh fruit for day trips.

✳

Travlzi tip: book mountain days ahead

Paragliding is a memorable way to see the valley in suitable weather. Book with a reputable local operator, follow the pilot’s safety guidance, and confirm conditions. For winter trips, reserve popular mountain transport early and keep a flexible backup plan for changing weather.
