Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Travlzi ("Travlzi", "we", "us" or "our") handles information when you visit travlzi.com. We aim to collect as little information as possible through this website.

1. Information you enter on this website

The Travlzi travel helper is currently a simple, browser-based guide. When you type into its chat box, your message and the displayed chat are handled in your browser. The current chat does not send your message to a Travlzi server, store it in a Travlzi account, or automatically forward it to WhatsApp. The chat is cleared when the page is reloaded or closed.

The website does not currently provide an account sign-up, booking checkout, payment form, or contact form.

2. WhatsApp and other external services

If you choose to follow a WhatsApp link or contact us, you leave this website and interact with WhatsApp, which is operated by Meta. WhatsApp may process your phone number, messages, and other information under its own terms and privacy policy. We receive information you choose to send to our business account; please avoid sending passwords, payment-card details, or other highly sensitive information.

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and travel images from Unsplash or image files hosted on GitHub. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details when your browser requests their resources. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests.

3. Product suggestions and retailer links

The Travel Essentials section contains Travlzi-written suggestions and links to search results on third-party retail websites, including Amazon India and Flipkart. These links open the retailer's website in a new tab. Travlzi does not copy or host retailer product photographs, catalogue descriptions, prices, or stock information in this section. Retailers may change their listings, prices, availability, delivery terms, and privacy practices at any time. Any purchase is made directly with the retailer and is subject to that retailer's terms. Travlzi does not currently claim or receive affiliate commissions from these links.

4. Cookies and analytics

The current Travlzi website does not intentionally set advertising cookies or use an analytics tool. Your browser or the website hosting provider may process limited technical information needed to deliver and protect the website.

5. How we use information

If you contact Travlzi through WhatsApp, we use the details and travel information you choose to share to respond to your enquiry and discuss travel planning. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Data storage and security

The current website chat does not create a Travlzi-side message record. Messages you send through WhatsApp are handled within WhatsApp and the business account. We take reasonable care with information shared with us, but no online service can promise absolute security.

7. Children and family use

Our website is intended for general travel-planning information, not for collecting children's personal information. If you are under 18, please involve a parent or guardian before sharing personal details or contacting us about a trip.

8. Your choices

You can choose not to use the chat or external contact links. You can also manage WhatsApp privacy settings through WhatsApp. If you have already contacted us and want to ask about information you shared, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or its features change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

10. Contact Travlzi

For privacy questions, contact Travlzi on WhatsApp at +91 81477 28538.

Note: If we add online bookings, forms, analytics, or automated WhatsApp messaging in the future, this policy should be reviewed and updated to describe those features before they go live.
